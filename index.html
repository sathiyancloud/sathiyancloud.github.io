What you’ll find here

The order is deliberate: concepts → practice → how it shows up at work. Labs only click once the vocabulary makes sense; job and interview context lands better after you’ve actually run commands and read logs.

1 · Core concepts

Linux, networking, and automation—not as trivia, but tied to how teams build, ship, and operate software.

2 · Hands-on practice

Short exercises and realistic troubleshooting so terminals, errors, and containers feel normal before you touch anything production-like.

3 · Roles & interviews

What entry jobs involve, how to describe your projects, and what interviews often stress—without buzzword stuffing.
